# Accessibility statement

This statement applies to Secblitz 1.0.0 and was written on 2026-10-09.

We want everyone to be able to use Secblitz. This page says plainly what works today and what does not yet.

## What works

- **Keyboard.** You can use all of Secblitz without a mouse. Tab and Shift+Tab move between buttons and options, Enter and Space press them, the arrow keys move within a group, Esc closes a window, and Ctrl+F searches.
- **Animation.** Secblitz follows the "Animation effects" setting in Windows. If you turn it off, Secblitz shows less movement.
- **Display size.** Secblitz follows the display scaling you chose in Windows, so text and buttons grow with it.
- **Light and dark.** Secblitz has a light and a dark look. You choose between them in Settings.
- **Languages.** Secblitz is available in English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Italian.

## What does not work yet

- **Screen readers.** Screen readers such as Narrator and NVDA cannot read Secblitz yet. Secblitz is built with a toolkit (iced) that does not support screen readers so far. We would like to fix this and will do so when the toolkit allows it.
- **High contrast.** Secblitz has no high contrast theme yet. It does not follow the Windows high contrast themes.

## Tell us about a problem

If something in Secblitz is hard for you to use, please tell us. Open an issue at https://github.com/secblitz/Secblitz/issues or write to support@secblitz.lol. Say what you were trying to do and what got in the way. You do not need technical words.
